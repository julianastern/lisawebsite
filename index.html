About Lisa

Professional Summary

Lisa Herzog is an accomplished advancement professional with expertise in architecting and leading comprehensive fundraising campaigns. Currently serving as Deputy to the Chief Advancement Officer & Campaign Director at National Geographic Society, she has driven transformative growth in resource development through building cultures of innovation, collaboration, and measurable success.

Her leadership at National Geographic Society includes launching and directing the quiet phase of a $1B campaign, overseeing all facets of strategy, execution, and performance. Through the creation of the Society's first Advancement Operations team and implementation of data-informed donor engagement models, she has positioned the organization for lasting philanthropic momentum.

Lisa's approach combines strategic vision with operational excellence, resulting in record-breaking fundraising achievements and sustainable organizational growth.
