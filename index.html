About Lisa

Strategic leader with 20+ years of expertise in fundraising, campaign management, and organizational development

Lisa Herzog is a passionate fundraiser with 20 years of experience in the nonprofit sector, currently serving as Deputy to the Chief Advancement Officer & Campaign Director at National Geographic Society. She has driven transformative growth in resource development through building cultures of innovation, collaboration, and measurable success.

Lisa began her career at the George Washington University where she helped create and launch a mid-level giving society. More than a decade later, The Luther Rice Society continues to thrive as a nationwide community of GWU supporters and as a critical pipeline for leadership gifts.

In 2012, Lisa joined the Brookings Institution where she managed primarily unrestricted giving – from individuals, corporations and foundations - throughout a $650M campaign, established a young leadership giving society and spent her final year serving as the Director of West Coast Initiatives.

In 2018, Lisa joined Wolf Trap Foundation for the Performing Arts, where she managed the Leadership Giving program and helped to direct foundation-wide fundraising strategy and efforts.

After working Wolf Trap, Lisa joined National Geographic Society. At National Geographic Society, Lisa's leadership included launching and directing the quiet phase of a $1B campaign, overseeing all facets of strategy, execution, and performance. Through the creation of the Society's first Advancement Operations team and implementation of data-informed donor engagement models, she has positioned the organization for lasting philanthropic momentum.

Lisa's approach combines strategic vision with operational excellence, resulting in record-breaking fundraising achievements and sustainable organizational growth.
