about_para1: "Lisa Herzog is a passionate fundraiser with 20 years of experience in the nonprofit sector, who most recently served as Deputy to the Chief Advancement Officer & Campaign Director at National Geographic Society. She has driven transformative growth in resource development through building cultures of innovation, collaboration, and measurable success."
about_para2: "Lisa began her career at the George Washington University where she helped create and launch a mid-level giving society. More than a decade later, The Luther Rice Society continues to thrive as a nationwide community of GWU supporters and as a critical pipeline for leadership gifts."
about_para3: "In 2012, Lisa joined the Brookings Institution where she managed primarily unrestricted giving – from individuals, corporations and foundations - throughout a $650M campaign, established a young leadership giving society and spent her final year serving as the Director of West Coast Initiatives."
about_para4: "In 2018, Lisa joined Wolf Trap Foundation for the Performing Arts, where she managed the Leadership Giving program and helped to direct foundation-wide fundraising strategy and efforts."
about_para5: "After working at Wolf Trap, Lisa joined National Geographic Society. At National Geographic Society, Lisa's leadership included launching and directing the quiet phase of a $1B campaign, overseeing all facets of strategy, execution, and performance. Through the creation of the Society's first Advancement Operations team and implementation of data-informed donor engagement models, she has positioned the organization for lasting philanthropic momentum."
